About SocialFuse

The ultimate hub for multi-tenant cross-platform publishing and smart comment moderation.

SocialFuse was created to empower content creators, digital marketers, and brands with the tools they need to automate scheduling and engage communities safely across multiple networks. We integrate directly with leading platforms to provide absolute scheduling freedom combined with intelligence filters.

Core Offerings

📅 Multi-Platform Scheduling
Draft, preview, schedule, and publish posts to Instagram, LinkedIn Profiles/Pages, and Reddit subreddits.
🛡️ Smart Toxicity Filtration
Filter toxic, offensive, or promotional comments automatically using high-accuracy AI models.
💡 Sarcasm & Sentiment Scan
Go beyond literal keywords. Our AI detects context, sarcasm, and real-time sentiment polarity.

Our Mission

Our goal is to build secure, frictionless bridges for modern workflows. We prioritize per-user OAuth tokens (multi-tenant architecture) to guarantee that you keep control over your digital footprint. By automating repetitive tasks, SocialFuse lets you focus on what really matters—building your community and brand.
